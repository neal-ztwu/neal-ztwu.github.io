01 / Selected work

Research

2026 · Preprint

Direct Translation between Sign Languages

Built direct ASL/CSL/DGS translation without a spoken-text pivot at inference and created 112K cross-lingual pairs through back-translation. On held-out back-translated inputs across six directions, reduced mean motion error (DTW-PA-MPJPE) by 19% and achieved 2.3× faster single-GPU inference than a three-stage cascade.

Synthetic data & curation · multilingual generation · mBART · VQ-VAE

To NAACL 2027

Simultaneous Translation between Sign Languages

Built streaming sign-to-sign generation and proposed CA-Stream-AL to measure computation-aware latency. Reduced latency by 38% versus full-sentence decoding, with 9% higher motion error (DTW-PA-MPJPE) and 2.1 points lower BLEU-4.

Evaluation design · streaming inference · wait-k · multi-path training

2025 · Preprint

Geometry-Aware Text-to-Sign Generation

Built variable-length 3D sign generation with geometry-aware objectives for articulated hands and consistent skeletons. Reached 14.22 back-translation BLEU-4 on PHOENIX14T development data, 3.08 points above the XLM-R baseline.

3D human motion · XLM-R · OpenPose · inverse kinematics
