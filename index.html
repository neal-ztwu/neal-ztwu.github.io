01 Submitted to NeurIPS 2026 · Preprint

Direct Translation between Sign Languages

Direct ASL/CSL/DGS translation without a spoken-text pivot at inference, replacing a three-stage cascade.

mBARTVQ-VAETranslateGemma-4BBack-translationQwen3-8B

Paper / arXiv ↗

Problem
Remove text-pivot information loss, error propagation, and three-pass latency from sign-to-sign translation.
Techniques
Created 112,324 cross-lingual pairs from How2Sign, CSL-Daily, and PHOENIX-2014T; jointly trained text-to-sign and sign-to-sign tasks with a frozen motion tokenizer.
Results
On the held-out BT-input test set, reduced mean DTW-PA-MPJPE by 19%, improved back-translation BLEU-4 by 50%, and achieved 2.3× faster inference than the cascade.
02 Submitted to EMNLP 2026

Simultaneous Translation between Sign Languages

Problem. Generate target-sign motion before the complete source utterance is available for streaming ASL/CSL/DGS translation.

Techniques. Combined mBART-large-cc25 with a frozen VQ-VAE over SMPL-X poses; implemented prefix-to-prefix training, test-time wait-k decoding, stochastic multi-path supervision, and CA-Stream-AL.

Results. Reduced computation-aware latency by 38% versus full-sentence decoding, with a measured trade-off of 9% higher DTW-PA-MPJPE and 2.1-point lower BLEU-4.

03 arXiv preprint

Geometry-Aware Text-to-Sign Generation

Problem. Generate variable-length 3D sign-pose sequences from text while preserving hand articulation and skeletal geometry.

Techniques. Used XLM-R with OpenPose, 2D-to-3D lifting, inverse kinematics, skeleton normalization, parent-relative weighting, bone-aware losses, and explicit EOS prediction.

Results. Reached 14.22 back-translation BLEU-4 on PHOENIX14T dev (+3.08 points over XLM-R) and reduced bone-length and movement-variance discrepancies by 18.76% and 5.48%.
